# ADR-0001: Template for architecture decisions

- Status: Proposed
- Date: 2026-11-12

## Context

Describe the problem, constraints, and architectural drivers.

## Decision

Describe the decision that is being made.

## Rationale

Explain why this decision matches the goals of SentinelOps and the project specification.

## Alternatives considered

List the alternative approaches and why they were rejected or deferred.

## Consequences

Document the expected benefits, trade-offs, dependencies, and security implications.

## Cost and operational impact

Describe expected costs, dependencies, and cleanup assumptions.

## Follow-up actions

List any required documentation, tests, or implementation tasks tied to this decision.
